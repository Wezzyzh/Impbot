IMP — Work Edition Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Creator, community administration and scope

IMP — Work Edition is created and maintained by woody. Each Root community that installs IMP manages its own installation through its owner and authorized administrators. Community administrators are the first contact for privacy and data requests concerning their installation. For developer assistance or escalation, contact wuki.tera@gmail.com. This policy describes IMP's community support, moderation, welcome-message, notification and temporary voice-room features. Root's own platform practices are described separately at https://www.rootapp.com/privacy-policy.

Information processed

Depending on the features enabled and permissions granted by your community, IMP processes:

- Root user and community identifiers, display names, profile-image references, membership dates and role information needed for authentication and staff access.

- Support-ticket subjects, categories, descriptions, replies, assignments, status changes, participant names and timestamps.

- Moderation targets, staff identifiers, reasons, action outcomes and applicable expiry times; native ban information where Root provides it.

- Welcome-message settings, observed membership events and generated welcome messages.

- Notification recipients, related tickets, timestamps, read status and delivery outcomes.

- Announcement content, scheduling information and the staff member responsible.

- Temporary voice-room names, creator and invited-member identifiers, privacy settings, channel identifiers, occupancy counts and operational records.

IMP can read recent channel messages when authorized staff use chat-cleanup tools. IMP does not access or record voice audio. Do not put passwords, payment details or unnecessary sensitive information in tickets or moderation reasons.

Purposes

IMP uses this information to authenticate users, enforce permissions, provide private support, carry out authorized moderation, send welcome messages and announcements, deliver notifications, manage temporary voice rooms, and record action outcomes to prevent duplicate operations and support review.

IMP does not include advertising or independent analytics integrations, sell personal data, or use community content to train AI models.

Storage and access

In production, IMP uses Root's hosted app runtime and per-community app datastore. Local development uses a database on the developer's computer. Root hosts and processes app data under its own policies and developer terms.

Ticket contents are accessible to the ticket creator and authorized support, moderation and administrative staff in that community. Private moderation notices are accessible to the affected member and authorized moderation staff. Other records are shown according to the app's staff permissions. Welcome messages and announcements sent into community channels are visible according to Root's channel permissions.

Authorized users may download ticket transcripts to their own devices. Those downloaded copies are outside IMP's datastore and are not automatically removed by changes within IMP. Root may access hosted app data as described in its own policies. Authorized community staff access their installation according to its permissions. Creating IMP does not automatically give woody access to other communities or their installation data. Developer assistance may require coordination with the community and Root.

Retention

Closing a ticket does not delete it. IMP does not automatically delete ticket content or moderation history after a fixed period. These records are retained until deletion is requested and processed, unless earlier deletion is required by applicable law, Root's instructions or developer terms, or because the data is no longer necessary for the app's stated functionality. Uninstallation or discontinuation of the app is also subject to Root's data-deletion requirements. Some operational records have capacity limits, and deleted voice-room records are periodically pruned; these are separate from ticket and moderation retention.

Each installing community's owner and authorized administrators are responsible for managing their use of IMP, reviewing the need to retain their installation's records, and coordinating valid correction or deletion requests. Any deletion performed within an installation is limited to that community's IMP datastore. This allocation of day-to-day tasks does not override applicable law, Root's developer terms, or the developer's obligations. Root controls its platform records and backups. A moderation expiry or deletion of a native channel does not automatically erase all related IMP records.

Privacy and deletion requests

For access, correction or deletion of community-specific IMP data, first contact the owner or administrators of the Root community where you used IMP. Each installing community is responsible for receiving and coordinating requests concerning its installation. Identify the community and your Root account; never provide passwords or developer tokens.

If the community cannot resolve your request, contact the developer, woody, at wuki.tera@gmail.com. The developer can help coordinate with community administrators and Root, but does not automatically have access to other communities' app datastores. Community administrators may need to verify your identity before acting.

IMP includes an administrator-only Settings → Data deletion tool for removing stored records linked to a selected member. Administrators can search by name or member ID, review affected record counts, and explicitly confirm permanent deletion. It removes whole ticket conversations involving that member, including other people's replies, and linked moderation, notification, welcome, scheduling and operational records. Active owned voice rooms and unresolved linked voice operations must be handled first. Personal information in unrelated free text requires separate review. Closing a ticket or removing a member alone does not erase their IMP records.

The deletion tool affects only the selected community's IMP app datastore. It does not delete your Root account, native Root chat messages, native roles, bans or voice permissions, records in other communities, or ticket transcripts previously downloaded to people's devices. Root profile information remains available through Root while you are a community member, and future activity may create new IMP records. Root controls its own platform records and backups. Some requests may require retention or anonymization under applicable law or Root's instructions; any limitation should be explained to the requester.

For your Root account or data controlled separately by Root, contact Root through its own support channels. Nothing in this policy transfers or waives responsibilities imposed by applicable law or Root's terms.

Changes

The developer will update this policy when IMP's data practices change. Installing communities should make the current policy and their administrator contact method accessible to members.
