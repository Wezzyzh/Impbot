# Privacy Policy for IMP (Stage 1)

Last updated: 11 October 2026

## Operator and contact

Root hosts the published app and its datastore. The app operator remains responsible for the app's behavior and for meeting the developer privacy and security obligations. Root's platform processing is described in its [Privacy Policy](https://www.rootapp.com/privacy-policy).

## Data and purpose

IMP uses information provided by Root to display welcome messages, community membership statistics, and a history of new members joining. It processes Root user IDs, display names, profile-picture references, community information, available online status, and role or owner information used to determine staff access.

For each recorded join, IMP stores the member's Root user ID, display name, profile-picture reference, the time the join was recorded, and the generated welcome message. Online status is used for current statistics and is not stored in this join history.

## Visibility and services

The join history, including member names, profile pictures, recorded join times, and welcome messages, is visible to community members who can access the app. It is not restricted to staff.

IMP uses Root's APIs and configured app data store. This Stage 1 version contains no integration with an AI service, advertising network, or analytics service, and no feature that reads or stores chat messages. Root may separately process platform data under its own privacy policy.

## Retention and requests

Join records are stored in the app's persistent database. This version has no automatic deletion period and does not delete join records when a member leaves the community. Records remain until manually deleted or the app's database is removed. Platform backups may follow separate retention rules.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your information, email **herzog.luca@hotmail.com**. Include your Root user ID and community name so the relevant records can be located. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional privacy rights.
